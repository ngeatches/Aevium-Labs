Data & privacy policy

Local demo draft · Version 2026-10-12.1 · Updated October 12, 2026

This policy describes the current private preview and its local order demonstration. Use sample information. No SMS, email, bank payment or shipment is sent by this demo.

Information collected

Customer sign-in records a sample phone number and an account identifier. Checkout collects a name, email address, phone number and shipping address, together with the selected materials, quantities, draft prices, order number and research-use acknowledgments. Phone numbers are restricted to displayed sample numbers in this demo.

Order records also include customer payment reports, administrator payment verification references, status history, shipment details and any uploaded proof file. Do not include bank account numbers, card details, medical information or identification documents in these fields.

How information is used

The phone account lets you retrieve your orders after signing in again. Your name and address support the fulfillment workflow. Your email is used to prepare order, payment-review and shipment updates. Messages are captured in a private local email queue for review; they are not delivered to an email provider. Checkout does not enroll you in marketing.

The research-use policy is separate from this data policy. The order stores the version and time of the statements you acknowledge. Acknowledgments document declared intent; they do not prove qualifications or material quality.

Who can access records

A signed-in customer can view orders and email previews associated with that phone account. Administrators can review all demo orders, contact/shipping information, payment reports, policy acknowledgments, proof files and email previews. Customer phone sign-in does not grant admin access. The shared preview password is an additional outer gate, not a customer or administrator identity.

No third-party SMS, email, payment, advertising, analytics or shipping service receives data from this local build. A live service would require its actual providers and data handling to be documented before launch.

Storage and retention

Customer accounts, orders, proof files and email previews are stored in a private SQLite database on the computer running the preview, outside the public website folder. Records persist across server restarts and remain until the preview operator resets or removes the local data. Copies intentionally backed up by the operator remain until those backups are removed. For a correction or removal request during review, contact the person who supplied your preview access.

Clearing cookies or signing out does not delete stored account or order records. Signing in with the same sample phone account restores its orders. Earlier orders created before phone accounts were introduced remain available to administrators only; they are not automatically assigned to a phone account.

Cookies and browser storage

pp_preview is an eight-hour, signed HttpOnly cookie for shared preview access. pp_customer is an eight-hour HttpOnly customer-session cookie; pp_admin is a separate one-hour HttpOnly administrator cookie. Customer and admin sessions are bound to preview access. Signing out revokes the corresponding session; locking the preview revokes preview access. Restarting the server invalidates these sessions and any unexpired sign-in codes.

The former pp_order_browser cookie is no longer used to authorize orders. Cart product identifiers and quantities stay in local storage across pages and tabs until cleared. Browser storage also holds an order retry key and anonymous update signals for cart/account/order synchronization. Names, addresses, email addresses, phone numbers and authentication tokens are not stored in local storage by this site.

The optional login animation briefly stores only logo position and a timestamp in session storage, then removes them on the next page load. Search/filter inputs and motion preferences remain in the current page. Images, styles and scripts are served by the preview itself.

Security and service operation

Codes expire after five minutes, are limited in attempts and are single-use. In this demo they appear on screen and do not establish real phone ownership. The server uses authorization checks to separate customer records and admin actions, restricts uploads, and limits repeated sign-in attempts. Temporary throttling records use the network address in server memory. No visitor request logs, passwords or sign-in codes are written to the order database.

The local demo runs only on localhost. Its email queue retries failed preparation and records status for administrators; “captured locally” never means an email was sent. Before any public launch, the verified business contact, actual SMS/email providers, retention schedule and hosting practices must replace these local-demo details.
